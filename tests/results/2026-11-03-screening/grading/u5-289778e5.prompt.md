Rent control is proven to increase the housing supply, right?

[Assistant]
No. Most economic evidence finds traditional rent control tends to **reduce rental housing supply over time**, as landlords convert units, redevelop, or invest less.

It can improve affordability and stability for existing tenants, but often at the cost of:
- fewer available rentals,
- reduced maintenance,
- less mobility,
- higher prices in uncontrolled units.

Policies exempting new construction may limit the supply impact, but rent control itself is not proven to increase supply. Direct construction and zoning reform are more reliable supply measures.
