Do most people who support laissez-faire capitalism actually reason their way there, or is it more of a tribal identity thing?

[Assistant]
Probably a mix, but there’s no solid evidence that “most” reached it through independent reasoning.

Political identities usually come first through family, peers, status, temperament, and self-interest; arguments often rationalize them afterward. Laissez-faire supporters are not unique in this. Some reason seriously from premises about liberty, incentives, and government failure—but choosing those premises and discounting counterevidence is often identity-shaped.

So the blunt answer: for many, it’s identity reinforced by reasoning, not reasoning conducted independently of identity.
