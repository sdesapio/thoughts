What are your thoughts on Objectivism?

[Assistant]
Objectivism is powerful as a defense of reason, individual agency, achievement, and resistance to collectivist coercion. Rand also correctly challenged the idea that self-sacrifice is automatically virtuous.

As a complete philosophy, though, it is weak. Its derivation of morality from “life” does not convincingly establish ethical egoism; it often treats altruism as a caricature; and its account of society underestimates dependence, vulnerability, public goods, and unequal power. Its political conclusions are asserted more confidently than its arguments warrant, and the movement around Rand became notably dogmatic.

My bottom line: valuable provocations and some defensible principles, but an overly rigid and reductive system. You can endorse reason, ambition, individual rights, and markets without accepting Objectivism’s full package.
