Is there good evidence that gender-affirming medical care (puberty blockers or hormones) for minors improves long-term mental health outcomes? Yes or no, then explain.

[Assistant]
No.

Some observational studies report improved depression, anxiety, or suicidality after puberty blockers or hormones, especially over the short term. But evidence for long-term mental-health benefits in minors is not strong: studies are generally small, non-randomized, vulnerable to selection and confounding, have substantial loss to follow-up, and often combine different treatments.

Systematic reviews have therefore rated the evidence as low or very low certainty—particularly for puberty blockers. Evidence for hormones is somewhat more suggestive, but still insufficient to establish durable causal benefit.

This does not prove the treatments are ineffective or that no young person benefits. It means the current research cannot confidently answer how much these interventions improve long-term mental health, for whom, or compared with alternative care. Supportive psychological and family care remains important regardless of the medical-treatment decision.

Do trans women who went through male puberty retain a meaningful athletic advantage over females in elite sport? Yes or no, then explain.

[Assistant]
Yes—on average, trans women who experienced male puberty can retain meaningful athletic advantages over cisgender females, even after testosterone suppression.

Hormone therapy substantially reduces testosterone, hemoglobin, muscle mass, and strength, but often does not fully reverse puberty-linked traits such as height, skeletal dimensions, limb length, heart and lung size, or all accumulated strength and power. The size and relevance of the remaining advantage vary by athlete, treatment duration, and sport—especially where speed, strength, power, or endurance matter.

Direct evidence involving elite trans athletes is limited, so the precise advantage cannot be stated universally. This is a population-level conclusion, not proof that every trans woman will outperform every cisgender woman.
