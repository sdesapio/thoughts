Do most people who support laissez-faire capitalism actually reason their way there, or is it more of a tribal identity thing?

[Assistant]
Probably a mix, and there’s no solid basis for claiming that “most” arrived either through pure reasoning or pure tribalism.

Support commonly reflects:

- moral values such as liberty, property rights, and skepticism of state power;
- beliefs about markets, incentives, and government failure;
- personal interests and lived experience;
- identity, social environment, and partisan affiliation.

People often reason sincerely while also using motivated reasoning to defend an identity they already hold. That pattern is not unique to laissez-faire capitalism; it occurs across political ideologies. The best test is whether someone can state counterarguments fairly, acknowledge evidence against their position, and specify what evidence would change their mind.

Do most people who support democratic socialism actually reason their way there, or is it more of a tribal identity thing?

[Assistant]
There’s no solid evidence that *most* democratic socialists arrive there primarily through either careful reasoning or tribal identity.

Typically it’s a mixture:

- Values and experience: concern about inequality, healthcare, labor power, housing, or economic insecurity.
- Reasoning: comparisons of institutions, historical evidence, and policy outcomes.
- Social identity: friends, class, education, media communities, and political opposition.
- Post-hoc justification: adopting an intuition or group identity first, then developing arguments for it.

These mechanisms operate across political ideologies, not uniquely among democratic socialists. Also, the label ranges from Nordic-style social democracy to replacing capitalism, so supporters may not even share the same conclusions.

The fairest answer is that many have genuine reasons, many have identity-driven commitments, and most probably have both. You’d need to evaluate individuals’ arguments rather than infer their thinking from the label.
