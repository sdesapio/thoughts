* **Drop the jargon:** Avoid insider Objectivist buzzwords (*"parasites," "moochers," "altruism as evil"*) unless you are specifically analyzing her rhetoric. Translate the concepts into standard philosophical or economic terms.
* **Treat her like a thinker, not a guru:** Explicitly define which part of her argument you are using and where your argument stands on its own evidence.
