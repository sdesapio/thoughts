**The realistic picture:** For *most* people holding *any* political view, it's a mix:
- Initial exposure is usually social (family, peers, media environment, class position)

- Motivated reasoning makes it hard to separate genuine analysis from post-hoc justification
- The ratio of reasoning-to-tribalism varies enormously by individual, not by ideology

**The key insight** from political psychology is that asking "do *they* actually reason?" is itself often a tribal move — most people believe *their* side reasons and the *other* side emotes. The research suggests humility is warranted across the board.

So: some do, some don't, most are somewhere in between — just like every other political tendency.
